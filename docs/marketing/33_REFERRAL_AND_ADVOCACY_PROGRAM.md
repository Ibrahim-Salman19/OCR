## 🛡️ 5. Fraud Prevention & Verification Safeguards

To maintain integrity and prevent referral abuse:
1. **Minimum Execution Threshold**: Referral rewards are unlocked only when the referred team processes at least 500 pages of verified OCR jobs.
2. **Domain Deduplication**: Multiple user signups from the exact same corporate email domain count as a single qualifying team referral.
3. **GitHub Account Age Verification**: If using GitHub OAuth, accounts created under 6 months prior require manual verification.
4. **Rate Limiting**: An individual advocate is capped at generating a maximum of 25 active invitation links per day.
